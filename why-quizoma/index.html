WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

Built for assessment, not just for energy.

Most classroom quiz tools are designed around speed: tap fast, score high, next question. That is genuinely useful for waking a room up. Quizoma is designed around the other half of the job — the quiz you actually have to mark.

Get the app See all features

Four differences that matter in practice

1. Written answers are graded properly

This is the reason Quizoma exists. Short written answers and fill-in-the-blanks are graded against a rule you pick per question — exact, case-sensitive, typo-tolerant, keyword coverage, or numeric equivalence, so 7, seven and 7.0 are one answer. Anything the rule cannot settle is handed to you for review rather than silently marked zero. Read how smart grading works.

The usual approach is plain text comparison, which means a student who knows the answer and mistypes one letter gets the same mark as one who left it blank. That does not save you time — it creates work, because now you have to check everything.

2. It survives the school network

Answers are saved to the participant's device the moment they are given, and synced afterwards. A dead connection in the middle of a test is a delay, not a disaster. Anything built around a live buzzer needs the network up for the whole session by design. Joining does need a connection — after that, the room can lose the network without losing the quiz.

3. Free with nothing held back

There is no paid tier. Not "free up to a certain number of players", not a trial — there is no upgrade, because there is nothing being sold. No ads either, and no in-app purchases, so nothing in your lesson turns into a pitch aimed at students.

4. Speed does not change the score

Scores are not weighted by how fast somebody tapped. That sounds like a small detail and it isn't: speed scoring quietly rewards the confident guesser over the careful thinker, which is the opposite of what assessment is usually for.

Where Quizoma is not the right choice

If what you need is a five-minute, high-energy review game with a leaderboard and music, Quizoma is not trying to be that. Nor is it the answer if you need to build quizzes in a desktop browser, or pick a ready-made quiz out of a large public library. Those are real requirements and it is better to rule us out in a minute than after you have written thirty questions.

Concretely, today: quizzes are created in the Android app (browser creation is on the way), and everyone signs in with a Google account. Taking a quiz works in any browser on any device.

So when should you use it?

  • When the result matters — a class test, a homework check, a compliance check at work, anything you need a report for.
  • When the questions need writing, not tapping — short answers, fill in the blanks, "explain why".
  • When the network is unreliable and a failed round is not acceptable.
  • When there is no budget, and there is not going to be one.

It is free and there is nothing to cancel, so the cost of trying it on one class test is about ten minutes. See the full flow first, or get the app.
