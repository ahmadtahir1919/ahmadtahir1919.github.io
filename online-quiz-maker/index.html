Build

A whole quiz before your tea gets cold.

Single choice, multiple correct, true/false, written, fill in the blanks and polls — mixed freely. Start from a template, then fly through with keyboard shortcuts.

  • Timers, points, hints and explanations per question
  • Test, Practice or Speed drill in one click
  • Schedule a start and end time
Import & reuse

Last term’s questions, in 10 seconds.

Paste text or drop a .txt / .csv file and get a ready quiz. Keep your best questions in a Question Bank and drop them into any quiz.

  • A starter template to download
  • Duplicates skipped automatically
  • Shared with the app — save once, use anywhere
Grade

Thirty papers. A few minutes.

Identical answers are grouped into one card, so one key marks them all. “carbon dioxde” still gets the mark — you only judge what really needs a human.

  • Rapid Grade, by question, or one person at a time
  • Notes and quick comments on any answer
  • Marks stay drafts until you press Submit
Results

Know who needs help — before the next lesson.

Score spread, accuracy per question and who took it where. Download PDF, Excel or CSV reports, and announce results when you are ready — not a second before.

  • Results when the quiz ends, or when you announce
  • A warning before announcing while it is still open
  • Urdu text exports correctly to Excel
