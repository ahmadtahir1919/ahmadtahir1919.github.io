The Idea Behind Quizoma

Auto-Grading + Real Teacher Review

Most quiz apps mark an answer wrong for one typo or a word swapped for its synonym. Quizoma splits grading into two fair tracks instead.

A Track A: Typo-Tolerant Auto-Grading Instant

A close spelling still counts. Numbers match in any form ("7" = "seven" = "7.0"). Said it in different words? Still recognized.

Accepts Number Words 85% Typo Match
B Track B: You Grade It Yourself Zero False 0%

Essays and open-ended answers are never auto-marked wrong. They go straight to you — because that's a call only a person should make.
