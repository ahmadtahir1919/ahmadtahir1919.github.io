Testly ("the app") lets people create quizzes and polls and lets others take them. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

1. What we collect

DataWhyWhere it goes
Google account email address and display name (via Google Sign-In)To create and identify your account, show your name to people you share a quiz with, and let you sign in again.Our backend (Supabase). Not sold or shared for advertising.
Content you create — quizzes, questions, answers, poll options, group namesTo store and deliver your quizzes.Our backend (Supabase).

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or SMS. The camera is used only, and only while that screen is open, to scan a quiz QR code on your device; QR images are not uploaded.

3. Legal basis (EEA/UK users)

We process account data and content to perform our contract with you (providing the app), and usage/diagnostics data under our legitimate interest in improving and securing the app. You can object to analytics — see section 6.

4. Sharing

Your display name and quiz activity are visible to the owner of a quiz you take, and a quiz owner's name is visible to participants. We use the processors named above (Supabase, Google/Firebase, PostHog) to run the service. We do not sell personal data. We may disclose data if required by law.

5. Retention

Account data and content are kept until you delete your account. Deleting your account removes your account and the data tied to it (quizzes, questions, attempts, answers, feedback, device tokens). Poll votes you cast in other people's polls are kept but disassociated from your account (anonymised). Analytics/crash records held by our processors are retained according to their own retention windows and are keyed only to a pseudonymous identifier after deletion.

6. Your choices & rights

  • Delete your account in the app: menu → Delete Account. Or request deletion at /delete-account/.
  • Access, correction, export, objection: email [CONTACT EMAIL].
  • Notifications: turn them off in your device settings or in the app's Settings screen.

7. Children

Testly is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Google Sign-In requires an eligible Google account. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Access to backend data is restricted by row-level security so users can only reach their own data and data shared with them.
