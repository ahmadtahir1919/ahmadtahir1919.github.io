GET THE APP

Download Quizoma — free, and free of ads.

Build quizzes, share a room code, and let the marking take care of itself. Android 7.0 and newer. No paid tier, no in-app purchases, nothing to cancel.

Quizoma is launching on Google Play in October 2026. You can already take a quiz in your browser today — no app needed.

Coming soon on Google Play

Just taking a quiz? You don't need the app

If somebody has given you a room code, you can answer straight from the browser you already have open — on a phone, a laptop, an iPad, anything. Go to quizoma.com/take, type the code, and start. There is nothing to install.

The app is for the person creating the quiz and marking it afterwards.

What you need

  • Android 7.0 (Nougat) or newer — phone or tablet.
  • A Google account to sign in with. There is no separate Quizoma password to create.
  • An internet connection to sign in and to join a quiz. After that you can go offline — creating and answering keep working, and everything catches up when the network returns.

Quizzes are created in the Android app for now — browser-based creation is on the way. Taking a quiz already works everywhere, in any browser on any device.

What you get

Six question formats

Single choice, multiple correct, true/false, written short answer, fill in the blanks, and live polls — mixable in one quiz, up to 20 questions each.

Grading that understands a typo

Five matching rules per question, including numeric equivalence and keyword coverage. Nothing written is ever silently marked wrong.

Keeps working offline

Answers are written to the device first and synced after, so a dropped connection mid-quiz costs nothing. Signing in and joining by code do need a connection.

PDF reports

Scores, per-question breakdown, every answer as typed, and your comments — ready to file or share.

Price

Free. There is no premium plan, no per-seat cost, no participant cap behind a paywall and no in-app purchases. There are also no advertisements, in the app or on this site.

Privacy

Quizoma stores what it needs to run a quiz and produce your report, and does not sell personal data. The specifics are in the privacy policy, and any account can be removed from the account deletion page.
