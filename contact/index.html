GET IN TOUCH

One address, and a person on the other end.

There is no ticket system, no chatbot and no contact form that swallows your message. Quizoma has one email address, it is written below, and it is read by the people who build the app.

Email us

support@quizoma.com

Open your email app

If that button does nothing, your browser has no email app set up — copy the address instead and paste it into Gmail, Outlook or whatever you use.

We read everything and reply. Usually within a couple of days. Tell us what you were doing and what happened instead; a screenshot saves a round trip.

Before you write

Some things have a faster answer than an email does.

A question about using Quizoma

Cost, accounts, offline use, grading rules, reports and limits are all covered on the FAQ. If your question is not there, write to us — that is a gap worth knowing about.

Reporting content in a quiz

Email us with the subject Quizoma content report and include the quiz code or name, what the problem is, and a screenshot if you can. The full policy is in the Terms of Service.

Privacy and your data

What is collected, why, and how long it is kept is set out in the Privacy Policy. For a request about your own data, email us with the subject Quizoma privacy.

Deleting your account

The steps, and what happens to your quizzes and results afterwards, are on the account deletion page. Send the request from the Google address you signed in with so we can confirm it is yours.

Who you are writing to

Quizoma is built by CraftByte Studio. It is free, carries no advertising and has no sales team — so nobody is going to add you to a mailing list for having emailed us.
